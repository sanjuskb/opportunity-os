# Opportunity OS — Visual Direction & Spatial Design Blueprint

**Status:** Approved Creative Direction  
**Aesthetic Anchor:** Concept 4 (Neural Transit Atlas) + Concept 1 (Architectural Maquette)  
**Editorial Philosophy:** International Technology Platform, Architectural Precision, Uncluttered Editorial Spatial Storytelling.

---

## 1. Core Visual Tenets

### 1.1 Spatial Architecture & Negative Space
- **Zero Clutter Rule:** No viewport may contain more than one primary cognitive focus.
- **Generous Section Tracks:** Major narrative beats are granted generous vertical tracks (minimum 120px - 180px vertical breathing room).
- **Sticky Pinned Views:** Core storytelling chapters use sticky 1.5x - 2.5x viewport tracks where background indices and foreground architectural maquettes remain pinned while copy and features fluidly crossfade.

### 1.2 Surface & Atmospheric Hierarchy
- **Canvas Foundation:** Deep obsidian warm-black (`#080B10` / `#0B0F17`).
- **Surface Elevation:**
  - Base: `#0B0F17` (Deep floor)
  - Level 1: `#111622` (Subtle container surface)
  - Level 2: `#182030` (Tactile interactive card)
  - Level 3: `#1F293D` (Elevated modal / popover)
- **Borders & Chamfers:** Precision architectural hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`). No thick neon borders.
- **Glow & Cones:** Radial atmospheric cones restricted to the AI Core (`#8B5CF6`) and active transit conduits (`#06B6D4`).

### 1.3 Ecosystem Color Semantics
Color is exclusively semantic, never decorative:
- **Students (`#06B6D4` - Electric Cyan):** Career Passports, verified badges, tailored resumes, candidate applications.
- **Companies (`#3B82F6` - Sapphire Blue):** Hiring pipelines, verified company listings, recruiter discovery.
- **Universities (`#10B981` - Emerald Green):** Institutional accreditation, DigiLocker marks signing, cohort analytics.
- **Opportunities (`#F59E0B` - Amber Gold):** Live internships, research fellowships, stipend benchmarks.
- **AI Core (`#8B5CF6` - Royal Violet):** Central intelligence matching, neural routing, agent shuttles.

## 2. Typography System

### 2.1 Display & Editorial Headings
- **Font Stack:** `'Newsreader', Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif`
- **Hero Title:** `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`, line-height `1.05`, tracking `-0.035em` (tight, authoritative editorial silhouette).
- **Chapter Numbers:** Display mono/serif indices (`01 / 07`, `clamp(3rem, 8vw, 8rem)`, opacity `0.15` in background).

### 2.2 Functional UI & Telemetry
- **Font Stack:** `'Inter', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`
- **Body Large:** `1.125rem` (18px), line-height `1.65`, color `#94A3B8`.
- **Body Regular:** `0.9375rem` (15px), line-height `1.6`, color `#CBD5E1`.
- **Label / Micro:** `0.75rem` (12px), uppercase tracking `0.08em`, font-weight `600`, color `#64748B`.
- **Code / Metrics:** `'JetBrains Mono', 'Fira Code', monospace`.
